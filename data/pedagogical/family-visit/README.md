# Situation: Family Visit

## Learner goal
After completing this pack, the learner should be able to handle a basic family visit in Kirundi: arrive and greet, ask about family members, respond to questions about themselves, accept food/drink, and say goodbye properly.

## Core communicative functions
- Arriving and announcing yourself
- Greeting family members (with appropriate respect levels)
- Asking about family members' health/news
- Responding to questions about your own life
- Accepting or politely declining food/drink
- Expressing gratitude
- Saying goodbye after a visit

## Core vocabulary
- Umuryango (family)
- Urugo (home / homestead)
- Turabaramukije (we greet you — arrival greeting)
- Karibu / Ingo (come in)
- Amata (milk — often offered to guests)
- Murakoze (thank you)

## Register notes
- Family visits involve complex register shifts: respectful to elders, warm to peers, gentle to children
- In-laws require particularly careful respectful language
- Refusing food or drink requires careful politeness

## Cultural notes
- Visiting family is a significant social activity in Burundian culture
- Guests are expected and welcomed — the interaction has a rhythm
- Offering milk or a drink is traditional hospitality
- Asking about each family member individually is normal and expected
- Leaving too quickly can be perceived as rude
