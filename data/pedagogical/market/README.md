# Situation: Market / Shopping

## Learner goal
After completing this pack, the learner should be able to navigate a basic market interaction in Kirundi: greet the seller, ask about items and prices, negotiate simply, and complete a purchase.

## Core communicative functions
- Greeting the seller
- Asking what's available
- Asking the price
- Saying it's too expensive
- Negotiating / making a counter-offer
- Agreeing on a price
- Thanking and leaving
- Asking for quantity / weight

## Core vocabulary
- Igiciro (price)
- Birahenze (it's expensive)
- Ndagusavye (please / I ask you)
- Ngahe? (how much?)
- Ndagushimiye (thank you)
- (add more as you create content)

## Register notes
- Market interactions are generally polite but not overly formal
- Humor and friendliness are common in negotiation
- Respectful address is still expected, especially with older sellers

## Cultural notes
- Negotiating price is normal and expected in many market contexts
- Buying without greeting first is considered rude
- Quantities may be described differently than in Western markets (heaps, bundles, etc.)
