# Review Guidelines

## Who reviews

For now, the founder (native Kirundi speaker) is the sole reviewer and approver. Later, additional native speakers may be added.

## Review checklist

For every record, check:

### 1. Is the Kirundi natural?
- Does it sound like something a real Kirundi speaker would say in this situation?
- Is it idiomatic, not a literal translation from English or French?
- Would you say this yourself?

### 2. Is it free of Kinyarwanda leakage?
- Are there words, forms, or patterns that are Kinyarwanda rather than Kirundi?
- If a word exists in both languages, is it being used the Kirundi way?
- When in doubt, flag it and add a note.

### 3. Are the translations accurate?
- Does the English translation convey the same meaning (not word-for-word)?
- Does the French translation convey the same meaning?
- Are register and tone preserved in translation?

### 4. Is the register label correct?
- Does the tagged register match how the sentence would actually be perceived?
- Would this sentence be appropriate in the tagged context?

### 5. Is the difficulty label correct?
- Would a learner at this level realistically encounter or need this?
- Is the vocabulary and grammar appropriate for the tagged level?

### 6. Are explanations helpful?
- Would a beginner understand the explanation?
- Is it too long? (Keep under 2 sentences for beginner content.)
- Does it explain the right thing — meaning, usage, or cultural context?

### 7. For corrections: is the learner error realistic?
- Would a real English/French-speaking learner actually make this mistake?
- Is the correction clearly better (not just a style preference)?
- Does the explanation help the learner understand why?

### 8. For dialogues: is the flow natural?
- Do the turns follow logically?
- Are speaker roles consistent?
- Would this conversation actually happen?

## How to mark review status

- Set to `reviewed` after first pass
- Set to `approved` only when you are confident the record is correct, natural, and ready for learners to see
- Leave as `draft` if you're unsure and need to come back to it
- Add notes in the `notes` column for anything ambiguous

## Kinyarwanda leakage protocol

If you suspect Kinyarwanda leakage in a record:
1. Add `[KW_CHECK]` in the notes column
2. Do not approve it
3. Research or consult if unsure
4. Either fix it or remove it

## Batch review

When reviewing a full situation pack:
1. Read through all records for consistency
2. Check that register distinctions are coherent across the set
3. Check that difficulty progression makes sense
4. Look for gaps: are there obvious things a learner would need that are missing?
